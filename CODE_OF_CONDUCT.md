# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and focus on technical outcomes.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Personal attacks or doxxing.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after moderation requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions and comments that violate this code.
